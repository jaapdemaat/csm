    Overview

    In person: 16Online: 12Cost: freeBooking: most need itChecked: 18 Nov 2026

    These archives sit alongside the CSM and UAL libraries rather than replacing them. Many were set up by communities to keep histories that larger institutions overlooked, so where something is kept, and by whom, is part of your research too. Four national museum collections are included alongside them, so you can compare what gets kept, and how, by community archives and by national institutions.

    Before you go
    • Book ahead. Most archives need an appointment, some up to four weeks ahead. Email with a short description of what you're researching.
    • Check the hours on the day. Several are volunteer-run or open only one or two days a week.
    • Bring ID. The British Library and The London Archives register you for a reader's card before you can see originals.
    • Pencils only. Most reading rooms don't allow pens near original material.
    • Ask before photographing. Rules on photography and reuse differ for each collection.
